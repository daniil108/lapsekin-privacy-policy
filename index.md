# Privacy Policy — LapseKin

**Effective date: 8 November 2026**

[English](#english) · [Русский](#russian) · [Deutsch](#de-DE) · [Español](#es-ES) · [Français](#fr-FR) · [Italiano](#it) · [Português (Brasil)](#pt-BR) · [日本語](#ja) · [한국어](#ko) · [简体中文](#zh-Hans)

[Support](support.html)

This policy describes how LapseKin 1.0 for iPhone handles information.

## 1. Photos, face information, and videos

LapseKin uses the iOS photo picker to import the photos you select. It uses Apple frameworks on your device to detect facial landmarks and align frames for your video. Imported photos, derived face geometry, project files, and exported videos are stored in the app’s private storage on your device. LapseKin does not send this content to its developer or to Firebase.

When you use Share or Save to Photos, iOS sends a copy to the destination you choose. That copy is managed separately from the project and video stored in LapseKin.

## 2. Usage analytics

LapseKin uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and improve it. Analytics is enabled by default in version 1.0. It records events such as completing a photo import, photo analysis, or video export. Google may also process session and device information, the app version, an installation identifier, and approximate location inferred from the IP address. LapseKin disables collection of Apple’s IDFV and uses the Firebase Analytics SDK variant without IDFA.

LapseKin does not use this data for advertising or for tracking people across other companies’ apps and websites. See [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase privacy and security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 3. Purchases and support

LapseKin does not require an account. Apple processes the optional lifetime Pro purchase through the App Store. LapseKin uses StoreKit to check your entitlement and does not receive your payment card details.

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use it to respond to your request.

## 4. Retention and deletion

You can delete projects and exported videos in LapseKin, clear app storage in Settings, or remove the app and its local data. To remove copies saved in Photos or sent elsewhere, delete them at their destination.

Firebase analytics data is retained according to the configured Google Analytics retention settings and Google’s policies. We keep support correspondence as long as needed to handle the request. To ask about your data or request deletion, email us using the address below. Because LapseKin has no user accounts, we may need additional information to identify a particular analytics record.

## 5. Changes and contact

When we update this policy, we will revise the effective date at the top of the page. For privacy questions or requests, email [daniil.sencov@gmail.com](mailto:daniil.sencov@gmail.com).

LapseKin использует Google Analytics for Firebase, чтобы понять, как работает приложение для пользователей, и улучшать его. В версии 1.0 аналитика включена по умолчанию. LapseKin не требует учётной записи. Apple обрабатывает необязательную пожизненную покупку Pro через App Store. LapseKin проверяет право доступа через StoreKit и не получает данные вашей банковской карты.

Вы можете удалить проекты и экспортированные видео в LapseKin, очистить хранилище приложения в настройках или удалить приложение вместе с его локальными данными. Копии, сохранённые в «Фото» или отправленные в другое место, нужно удалить там отдельно.

### 1. 照片、面部信息和视频
